\begin{abstract}
Message retention does not by itself determine how quickly work recovers after a consumer fails, nor what a publication confirmation guarantees. We present an auditable, computationally reproducible case study of recovery policies, concurrency behavior, and acknowledgment costs in Redis Streams 7.4.2 and NATS JetStream 2.10.24. Measurements use single-replica deployments and 512-byte messages on a two-node Kubernetes homelab. The study comprises 300 randomized backlog-drain trials, 60 planned publication trials, 60 serial-publisher sensitivity trials, 120 consumer-process kills, and ten no-reclaimer controls. Conditional proofs distinguish residual acknowledgment timeout, recovery scheduling, consumer occupancy, stable-storage assumptions, and the external-effect/acknowledgment gap; selected model statements are checked with Lean 4.24.0. All active recovery trials completed. At a 250\,ms timeout, kills at 10\% and 75\% of the receipt-based interval were followed by median failure-to-redelivery intervals of 226.5 and 63.6\,ms for JetStream and 230.2 and 67.9\,ms for Redis with a 10\,ms reclaim sleep. Redis controls without reclamation remained pending. With sixteen consumers, mean acknowledged drain throughput was 8.59--12.94 times the one-consumer mean across profiles, alongside higher mean within-trial cycle p99 latency. In the sixteen-publisher workload, periodic configurations had approximately 83\% and 82\% of memory-mode throughput for Redis and JetStream, respectively. Always-synchronize profiles exhibited lower throughput and greater variability; one Redis-always timed trial failed and is retained as an outcome. Completed-run estimates condition on completion. Sensitivity diagnostics retain the direction of paired contrasts but expose influential observations; storage-history carryover prevents isolating synchronization-policy effects. Consumer-state persistence differs between products, and consumer kills do not validate power-loss durability. The versioned artifact retains raw timings, failures, configurations, proofs, and deterministic analysis for this bounded comparison.
\end{abstract}
