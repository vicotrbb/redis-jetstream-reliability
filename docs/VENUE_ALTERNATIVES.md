# Publication alternatives: four publisher-verified candidates

Access date: **2026-09-23**. Scope: the existing approximately 25-page Redis Streams/NATS JetStream empirical article and possible *distinct* software/data companion articles. This is a venue-fit assessment, not an acceptance prediction. No submission or publication was performed. Prices and journal statistics can change before submission/acceptance.

## Recommendation within this shortlist

**SN Computer Science is the strongest fit for the full paper and has a verified no-APC subscription option.** Its scope explicitly includes distributed/cloud computing and computer/network performance, and its description emphasizes sound science. It is therefore a defensible free journal fallback. SoftwareX and Data in Brief require a different contribution and article structure; JOSS has an immediate software-maturity barrier for this newly created artifact. None provides guaranteed acceptance or an immediate journal DOI.

## SN Computer Science

The [journal homepage](https://link.springer.com/journal/42979) identifies a **hybrid** journal, original-research scope, relevant systems/performance topics, sound-science assessment, free submission, no page/color charges, and the 96-day median first-decision metric. Its [current publishing/fee page](https://link.springer.com/journal/42979/how-to-publish-with-us) explicitly confirms that subscription publication has **no APC**; OA is optional after acceptance, with the prices in the table determined at acceptance. This is a current verification, not an assumption based on its previous publishing model.

The [submission guidelines](https://link.springer.com/journal/42979/submission-guidelines) specify single-anonymized review with at least two reviews, a structured 150–250-word abstract, and declarations. They explicitly state that Online First is citable with a DOI. No numerical manuscript page cap was identified in the inspected instructions. Springer Nature [permits preprints before/during review and does not treat them as prior publication](https://support.nature.com/en/support/solutions/articles/6000258807-preprints). Permission to retain a preprint should not be confused with unrestricted distribution of the typeset subscription article.

**Assessment (inference):** the paper can retain its research questions, results, limitations, formal model, and conclusion. Reformatting and a structured abstract are straightforward. The more substantive review risks are the single-homelab external-validity limit, historical broker versions, and differentiation from previous broker studies. The sound-science wording helps establish scope; it does not remove the need to defend the empirical contribution.

## SoftwareX

The accessible official [SoftwareX reviewer form](https://legacyfileshare.elsevier.com/promis_misc/softwarex-reviewer-form.pdf) evaluates an **original software publication**: research impact or reuse potential, empirical evidence, clear scientific scope, installability, an appropriate open-source license, documentation, maintainability, portability, and tests. This is a software contribution review, not simply a venue for any paper reporting software experiments. The form is a publisher-hosted legacy asset, not evidence that every administrative policy is unchanged today.

**Assessment (inference):** our existing comparative paper is not the natural submission. A separate article would need to make the reusable benchmark/failure-injection toolkit the contribution, with documented installation, extensibility, portable examples, licensing, and tests. A tool made only to collect this one study is a weaker proposition. This route requires appreciable repackaging and should not be sold as an easier publication of the existing article.

**Verification limits:** the official [journal page](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/softwarex), [author guide](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/softwarex/publish/guide-for-authors), and [insights page](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/softwarex/about/insights) returned access errors. Accordingly, no current fee, exact word/page limit, observed review-time metric, or publisher-hosted DOI example is asserted here. Third-party copies and historical APCs were deliberately not substituted. Elsevier's current [general preprint-sharing policy](https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/sharing) permits preprints, subject to any journal-specific exception; that exception check remains outstanding for SoftwareX.

## Data in Brief

The official [Data in Brief journal information indexed under Elsevier Journal Insights](https://journalinsights.elsevier.com/journals/2352-3409/downloads) describes short articles making research datasets discoverable and reusable, including replication, preliminary, proof-of-concept, and negative-result data. It provides the USD 1,600 list APC and four timing figures in the table. These facts were accessible in the live search index; subsequent full-page retrieval returned an access error. They should be rechecked directly before budgeting or submission. The inspected scope also excludes descriptions of data already published as supplementary material in an original research article: companion-publication sequencing deserves care.

Elsevier's official [quick guide](https://researcheracademy.elsevier.com/uploads/2024-07/Quick_guide_Data_in_brief.pdf) requires the data-article template and explains that this is not a shortened research article: interpretation, discussion, and conclusions do not belong in that format. The [co-submission explanation](https://www.elsevier.com/connect/research-data-publishing-made-easy-introducing-the-co-submission-process) describes repository deposition and linked companion articles.

**Assessment (inference):** the preserved raw timings, protocol, configurations, and incident ledger could support a useful dataset paper after curation and a stable public deposit. This would be a separate descriptive article; it would not publish the requested 25-page argument and conclusion unchanged. No publisher-hosted article DOI example was independently verified in this bounded pass. The general [Elsevier preprint policy](https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/sharing) is permissive; the journal-specific guide could not be fully inspected for exceptions.

Data in Brief is explicitly on Elsevier's [geographic-pricing pilot list](https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/pricing/gpoa-journals-list). The current [pricing policy](https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/pricing) places Brazil in the group paying **65% of list APC**. For an eligible author group based in Brazil, USD 1,600 × 0.65 would be **USD 1,040 before tax**. This is a conditional calculation, not a personalized quotation or a guarantee of eligibility; submission-system pricing controls.

## Journal of Open Source Software (JOSS)

Current [submission rules](https://joss.readthedocs.io/en/latest/submitting.html) require substantial research software, actual research use, an open license, and public active development over **more than six months**. A recent repository dump or one-off analysis tool is outside the intended scope. Review is public, with at least two reviewers. There is no submission/publication fee; accepted articles receive a **Crossref DOI**, separately from the required archived-software DOI. Preprints are welcome before, during, or after review. No overall acceptance-time median was verified.

The [paper requirements](https://joss.readthedocs.io/en/latest/paper.html) specify **750–1,750 words**, software-focused content, and an AI-usage disclosure. The article should explain the software and research need, rather than present new scientific results obtained using it.

**Assessment (inference):** this is not an immediate free route for the existing article or newly created repository. A later application would require sustained public development, a mature reusable package, evidence of research use, and a distinct short software paper. Six months of passive repository existence alone would not satisfy the development requirement.

## Decision boundary

For the complete empirical article, keep **SN Computer Science's subscription route** as the no-APC journal fallback. Treat SoftwareX/Data in Brief/JOSS as potential *different publications about reusable artifacts*, not alternatives that preserve the present manuscript unchanged. The broader recommendation should also consider the independently assessed PeerJ Computer Science and repository DOI routes; a repository DOI is not journal peer review.
